How to Play

Find an event, choose a market, build your ticket and follow the result from My Bets.

01

Browse events

Use Top for the most relevant events, Upcoming for the full schedule, Live for matches in progress and Starting Soon for the next kickoffs. Open All Sports when you want to filter by sport.

02

Open a match

Tap a match card to see its available markets.

03

Choose your odds

Tap an odds button to add the selection to your bet slip. Tap it again to remove it.

04

Choose Single or Multi

A Single is one bet on one pick. A Multi combines picks from different events. Every pick must win for the ticket to win.

05

Enter your stake

Open the stake field, enter an amount and check the total odds and possible win. Tap Place Bet only after the ticket looks right.

06

Follow your bets

Open My Bets to see confirmed tickets. Running holds active tickets. Bet History contains won, lost, void and refunded tickets after settlement.

07

Use Cash Out when offered

Some active tickets may receive a Cash Out offer. The amount can change with the match and is confirmed only when the cash out request succeeds.

08

Play BlazeBet Originals

Casino Originals put the stake, controls and result on one screen. Read the controls before you play. Every casino game can lose.
