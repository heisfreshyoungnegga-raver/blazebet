Platform, accounts and betting

Once BlazeBet accepts a bet the stake is committed. An accepted bet is not refundable, and a losing bet is not refunded.

A duplicate deposit, a wrong amount, or an amount credited in error is corrected once BlazeBet has confirmed it with the payment provider.

Contact support before asking a bank or payment provider to reverse a payment. An account used to raise a chargeback may be closed, the amount may be recovered from the balance, and bets funded by the disputed payment may be void.

1.6.Types of bets

Single: one stake on one selection.

Multi: one stake combining selections from different events. Every active selection must win for the full Multi to win. A void selection is normally recalculated at odds of 1.00.

A Multi may contain up to 30 selections. BlazeBet may prevent combinations that are related, unavailable, or not supported by the market provider.

System bets are not currently offered. If a new bet type is added, its rules will be shown before the Customer can place it.

Accepted bets cannot be edited or cancelled by the Customer. Cash Out may be used only when BlazeBet makes an eligible offer and confirms the request.

Maximum stakes, maximum potential returns, and market limits may vary by sport, event, market, account, payment risk, and available liquidity. The applicable limit is enforced before confirmation.

BlazeBet may lower or refuse a stake before acceptance. An already confirmed ticket remains subject to the rules and limits in force when it was accepted.

03

Placing and settling bets

3.1.Ticket confirmation

The Customer must check every selection, market, odds value, event, and stake before confirming a bet. The ticket stored in My Bets is the record of the accepted bet.

3.2.Pre-match and Live bets

Pre-match bets are accepted before an event starts. Live bets may be accepted after the start where BlazeBet offers a Live market.

Live prices, handicaps, totals, and available outcomes may change or pause at any time. A bet is not accepted until the ticket is confirmed.

3.3.Late bets

A pre-match bet accepted after the actual start of an event because of an error may be voided at odds of 1.00. Properly accepted Live bets remain valid.

3.4.Settlement and recalculation

BlazeBet settles tickets using the confirmed market, these Terms, any sport-specific rule, and the official result or approved data source.

If a settlement error is found, BlazeBet may correct the ticket and account balance. A correction will not affect unrelated tickets.

3.5.Result sources

Official competition records and approved sports data providers are used for settlement. If reliable sources conflict, settlement may be paused while the result is checked.

3.6.Voids and refunds

A void selection is settled at odds of 1.00. A void Single returns its stake. In a Multi, the void selection is removed from the odds calculation and the remaining selections continue.

3.7.Event integrity

BlazeBet may pause settlement where there is credible evidence of event manipulation, insider betting, incorrect data, or prohibited coordinated activity. Any action taken must relate to the affected activity and available evidence.

3.8.Taxes and deductions

Any tax or mandatory deduction required by law may be applied before payment. BlazeBet will not apply a winnings-release fee.
