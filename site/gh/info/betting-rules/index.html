Betting Rules

Market definitions and settlement rules for the sports available on BlazeBet.

Operator

TTS N.G.

Effective

25 July 2026

These Betting Rules explain how BlazeBet accepts and settles sportsbook markets. They apply together with the Terms & Conditions and any specific wording shown on the event, market or confirmed ticket.

Only markets displayed for an event are available. A rule describing a market does not mean that the market will be offered for every sport, competition or match.

The confirmed ticket records the accepted selection, odds, stake and market wording. If a sport-specific rule conflicts with a general rule, the sport-specific rule applies. If the event page clearly states a different settlement basis, that wording applies to that market.

01

Bet types and tickets

1.1.Single

A Single is one stake on one selection. A winning Single returns the stake multiplied by the accepted decimal odds.

1.2.Multi

A Multi combines selections from different events into one ticket. Every active selection must win for the Multi to win.

The combined odds are calculated by multiplying the accepted odds for each active selection. A void selection is recalculated at odds of 1.00 and the remaining selections continue.

A Multi may contain no more than 30 selections. BlazeBet may prevent related selections, selections from the same match, or combinations that the market provider does not support.

1.3.Unsupported bet types

System, complex, mix and combination-system bets are not currently offered. BlazeBet will publish separate rules before introducing another bet type.

1.4.Bet confirmation

Adding an outcome to the bet slip does not place a bet. A bet is accepted only when BlazeBet confirms it and creates a ticket in My Bets.

The Customer must review the event, market, selection, odds and stake before selecting Place Bet. Accepted tickets cannot be edited or cancelled by the Customer.

1.5.Void selections

A void Single returns its stake. A void selection in a Multi is settled at odds of 1.00.

1.6.Market labels

For three-way result markets:

  • 1 means the first or home participant wins.
  • X means the event ends in a draw.
  • 2 means the second or away participant wins.

The event page may use team names, player names or equivalent labels where that is clearer.

02

Core market rules

2.1.Match result

The Customer predicts the winner, or the draw where a draw is offered, for the playing time stated in the market.

A market called 1X2, Full Time Result, Match Result or Regulation Result normally excludes overtime, extra time and penalty shootouts. A market called Winner, Moneyline, To Qualify or Winner including overtime is settled on the wider playing period stated in its title.

2.2.Double chance

1X wins if the first participant wins or the event ends in a draw.

12 wins if either participant wins and the event does not end in a draw.

X2 wins if the second participant wins or the event ends in a draw.

2.3.Draw no bet

The Customer selects a participant to win. If the relevant playing period ends in a draw, the selection is void. If the selected participant wins, the selection wins. If the opponent wins, the selection loses.

2.4.Handicap

A handicap adds a stated advantage or disadvantage to a participant's score for settlement.

For a two-way handicap, the selection wins if the adjusted result favours the selected participant. It loses if the adjusted result favours the opponent. If the adjusted result is level on a whole-number handicap, the selection is void.

For a three-way European handicap, the available outcomes are first participant, handicap draw and second participant. One outcome wins and no void applies solely because the adjusted result is level.

2.5.Asian handicap

A quarter handicap splits the stake equally across the two neighbouring handicap lines.

For example, a stake on -1.75 is divided equally between -1.5 and -2.0. Each half is settled separately, then the returns are added together.

2.6.Totals

A total market asks whether a stated number of goals, points, games, sets, maps, rounds or another statistic will be over or under the displayed line.

If a two-way total finishes exactly on a whole-number line, the selection is void. Three-way totals that include an Exact outcome do not use this void rule.

Zero is an even number for Odd or Even totals.

2.7.Asian totals

A quarter total splits the stake equally across the two neighbouring total lines.

For example, Over 2.75 is divided equally between Over 2.5 and Over 3.0. Each half is settled separately.

2.8.Correct score

The Customer predicts the exact score at the end of the playing period stated in the market. Extra time and penalty shootouts are excluded unless the market says otherwise.

2.9.Half-time / full-time

The first label is the result at half-time. The second label is the result at the end of regular time.

For example, 2/1 means the second participant leads at half-time and the first participant wins at full-time.

2.10.To qualify

The Customer predicts which participant advances to the next round. Unless the market says otherwise, all legs of the tie, extra time and any penalty shootout are included.

2.11.Both teams to score

Yes wins if both teams score during the stated playing period. No wins if at least one team does not score.

2.12.First team to score

The Customer predicts which team scores first. If the market does not offer No Goal and no goal is scored, all team selections lose.

An own goal is credited to the team awarded the goal.

2.13.Goalscorer markets

Anytime Goalscorer wins if the selected player scores at least once during the stated playing period. Own goals do not count.

If the player takes no part, the selection is void. A substitute who enters the match remains active for Anytime Goalscorer.

For First Goalscorer, a player who enters only after the first valid goal is void. A player who took part before the first valid goal and did not score it loses. If an own goal is scored first, settlement moves to the next valid goalscorer unless the market states otherwise.

2.14.Team and player totals

Only the statistic named in the market is counted. A player who takes no part is normally void. A market may state a minimum participation requirement.

2.15.Period markets

Quarter, half, period, set, innings, map and round markets are settled only on the named segment. Later play does not affect a market that was already determined.

2.16.Race to a target

The Customer predicts which participant first reaches the displayed number of goals, points, games, rounds or kills.

If neither participant reaches the target, settlement follows any Neither outcome offered. If no such outcome was offered, selections are void unless the market wording states otherwise.

2.17.Outright and tournament markets

Outright markets are settled using the official final classification for the competition.

If multiple participants share a winning position and no tie-breaking outcome applies, the dead-heat rule is used. The stake is divided by the number of tied winners and the accepted odds are applied to that divided stake.

03

General settlement

3.1.Playing time

Markets are settled on regular time unless the title or sport-specific rule says that overtime, extra time, a shootout or another period is included.

Stoppage time forms part of regular time. Extra time does not.

3.2.Actual start time

The actual start time controls settlement. A pre-match bet accepted after the event started because of a delay or data error may be voided. Properly accepted Live bets remain valid.

3.3.Live information

Live scores, clocks, statistics and tracker graphics are provided for convenience. They can be delayed or corrected. The confirmed ticket and official settlement data control the bet.

Live odds, handicaps, totals and available outcomes may change, pause or close at any time.

3.4.Result sources

BlazeBet uses official competition records and approved sports data providers. If reliable sources conflict, settlement may be paused while the result is checked.

Results published by another betting operator do not control BlazeBet settlement.

3.5.Postponed events

If the event page does not state another rule, bets remain valid when an event is rescheduled to start within 48 hours of its original time.

If the event is not started within that period, affected selections are void. Tennis and similar tournament matches remain active until the match is completed in the same tournament or a participant withdraws.

3.6.Interrupted or abandoned events

Markets that were unambiguously determined before play stopped remain valid.

Other markets remain active if the event resumes and is completed within the applicable period shown on the event or by the data provider. If no period is shown, the default is 48 hours. If the event is not completed, undetermined markets are void.

An official result declared by the competition organiser may be used where the competition rules treat the event as completed.

3.7.Venue changes

A move to a neutral venue does not by itself void bets. If an event is moved to the opponent's home venue after bets were accepted, affected pre-match selections may be voided unless the venue change was already part of the competition arrangement.

3.8.Format changes

If the number or duration of periods, sets, maps, rounds or overs changes, only markets materially affected by that change are void. Match-winner markets remain valid when the official competition result still determines a winner, unless the market states a fixed format.

3.9.Technical results and disqualifications

A technical result awarded before play starts normally voids the event.

After play starts, markets already determined remain valid. Other markets are settled using the official result where the competition treats it as final, otherwise they are void.

3.10.Result corrections

Settlement normally uses the official result published immediately after the event. A later disciplinary change does not alter settlement unless the original result was recorded incorrectly, the market remained unsettled, or applicable law requires a correction.

3.11.Obvious errors

An obvious error can include a price, participant, score, market, start-time or format error that a reasonable Customer should recognise.

BlazeBet may void the affected selection or settle it using the correct market information where this can be established fairly. Unrelated tickets and selections are not affected.

3.12.Undetermined outcomes

If an event ends without enough information to determine a market, that market is void. Markets already determined remain valid.

04

Football

4.1.Regular time

Football markets are settled on 90 minutes plus stoppage time unless the market says otherwise. Extra time and penalty shootouts are excluded.

To Qualify, Lift the Trophy and equivalent advancement markets include extra time and penalty shootouts where used.

4.2.Cards

Unless the market says otherwise, cards shown to active players during regular time are counted. Cards shown after the final whistle, to coaches or to unused substitutes are excluded.

A player sent off after a second yellow normally contributes one yellow card and one red-card dismissal. A market may use a different card-points system where the values are displayed.

Cards shown during the half-time interval are assigned to the second half.

4.3.Corners, offsides and match events

A corner is counted when it is taken. An offside is counted when the official decision is made.

For minute-interval markets, first-half stoppage time belongs to the final minute of the first half and second-half stoppage time belongs to the final minute of regular time.

4.4.Goal method

A Header outcome requires the scorer's final touch to be with the head.

A Direct Free Kick outcome requires the goal to be scored directly from the free kick. A Penalty outcome requires a converted penalty kick.

Own goals count only for markets that specifically include them.

4.5.First or next event

The market is settled by the first valid occurrence of the named events. If neither event occurs, the outcome shown for that situation controls settlement. If no such outcome is offered, the market is void.

4.6.Substitutions

A substitution is counted when it is officially recorded. Simultaneous substitutions made before play restarts are treated as occurring at the same time.

If a market requires one team to make the first substitution and both teams substitute simultaneously, the market is void.

4.7.VAR

A VAR review counts only when the referee or official competition data records a review. A silent check that does not become an official review is not counted.

4.8.Comeback win

A comeback win occurs when a team trails at any point in regular time and later wins the match in regular time.

4.9.Tournament scorer

Top-scorer markets include goals scored in extra time and exclude penalty-shootout goals, unless the market says otherwise.

05

Tennis, table tennis and volleyball

5.1.Tennis scoring

Match handicaps and totals are measured in games unless the market states sets, points or another unit. A standard tie-break counts as one game.

A match tie-break or super tie-break used instead of a deciding set counts as one set and one game for settlement unless the market states otherwise.

5.2.Tennis retirement

If a player withdraws or is disqualified before the first point, all match selections are void.

After the match starts, markets already determined remain valid. Match-winner settlement follows the official player advancing only where the first set was completed. Other undetermined markets are void unless the market or provider rule states otherwise.

5.3.Tennis postponement and surface

A postponed tennis match remains active until it is completed in the same tournament. A change of court surface or venue does not by itself void bets.

5.4.Table tennis and volleyball

Match-winner markets use the official result. Handicaps and totals are measured in sets or points as stated.

If a participant withdraws or is disqualified after play starts, determined markets remain valid and undetermined markets are void unless the official competition records a completed result.

5.5.Next point and race markets

Next Point is settled on the next point completed after acceptance. Race to Points is settled when a participant first reaches the stated target.

06

Basketball, hockey and baseball

6.1.Basketball match markets

A two-way Winner or Moneyline market includes overtime unless the market says Regulation Time.

A three-way 1X2 market with a draw option is settled on regulation time. Quarter and half markets exclude overtime.

6.2.Basketball statistics

Match totals, handicaps and player statistics include overtime unless the market says otherwise. A player who does not take part is void.

3x3 Basketball is settled under the official competition result. Format differences do not void a match-winner market where an official winner is declared.

6.3.Hockey match markets

A three-way hockey result market is settled on regulation time.

A market called Winner including overtime and penalties includes overtime and any shootout used to determine the winner.

Period markets and regular-time player statistics exclude overtime and shootouts unless stated otherwise.

6.4.Hockey statistics

Shootout goals do not count toward team totals, player goal totals or correct score unless the market specifically includes them.

A player who does not participate is void.

6.5.Baseball

Baseball match-winner markets include extra innings unless the market says otherwise.

If an event is shortened, determined inning markets remain valid. Match and full-game markets are settled only where the competition declares an official completed result or the market's minimum-innings rule has been met. Otherwise they are void.

07

Rugby and cricket

7.1.Rugby playing time

Rugby union and rugby league markets are settled on 80 minutes unless the market says that extra time is included.

A moved venue does not void the event unless it is moved to the opponent's home venue or, for an international match, to another country after acceptance.

7.2.Rugby try markets

Penalty tries count toward team and match try totals. They do not count as a named player's first try.

A First Tryscorer selection is void if the player was not on the field when the first valid try was scored. An Anytime Tryscorer selection is void if the player takes no part.

7.3.Cricket official result

Cricket is settled using the official competition result. If the match is declared No Result, undetermined markets are void.

A result calculated under the competition's rain or reduced-overs method, including DLS, is an official result.

7.4.Cricket match winner

If a match ends tied and the market did not offer a tie, match-winner selections are void unless the competition declares a winner using a Super Over or another official tie-break.

7.5.Reduced overs

Markets already determined remain valid. Undetermined player and innings markets may be void where the scheduled number of overs is reduced, unless the market states that reduced overs are included.

7.6.Player markets

Only runs credited to the named batter count toward Batter Runs. Extras do not count.

A player not included in the starting XI is void. A player in the starting XI remains active even if they do not bat or bowl, subject to the specific market wording.

Best Batter and Best Bowler ties use the dead-heat rule unless an official tie-break is stated.

08

Boxing and MMA

8.1.Fight winner

The official bout result controls settlement. If a draw is offered, a draw wins only the Draw outcome. If no draw was offered and the bout ends in a draw, fighter-winner selections are void.

8.2.Method of victory

KO/TKO includes knockout, technical knockout, corner retirement and referee stoppage where the official result records a KO or TKO.

Submission includes tap-out, verbal submission and technical submission.

Decision includes the judges' decision after the scheduled rounds are completed.

Disqualification is settled under the method named in the official result or the specific market wording.

8.3.Winning round

The winning round is the round in which the official stoppage occurs. If a fighter does not answer the bell for the next round, the bout is treated as ending in the previous round.

8.4.Round totals

Half-round totals use the official elapsed time. In a five-minute MMA round, 2:30 is the half-round point. In a three-minute boxing round, 1:30 is the half-round point.

8.5.Format changes

If the number or duration of rounds changes, fight-winner and method markets remain valid where they are not materially affected. Round-specific and round-total markets are void.

8.6.No Contest

If the bout is declared a No Contest, markets already unambiguously determined remain valid. Other markets are void.

09

Esports

9.1.Match format

Bo1, Bo2, Bo3 and Bo5 describe the scheduled number of maps. Match-winner, map handicap and map total markets are settled using the official competition result.

If a match can end in a draw and no Draw outcome was offered, match-winner selections are void when no official winner is declared.

9.2.Line-up and technical changes

Player substitutions, server changes, rehosts and rollbacks do not by themselves void a team market.

If a team withdraws or is disqualified after play starts, determined markets remain valid. The official match winner may be used where the competition awards one. Other undetermined markets are void.

If the match is not completed within 48 hours of its original start, undetermined markets are void.

9.3.Overtime

Additional rounds used to complete a map are included unless the market offers a Draw outcome or says Regulation only.

9.4.Counter-Strike 2

Map winners, round handicaps, round totals and player-kill markets use the official CS2 match record.

The number of regulation rounds follows the tournament format. Overtime rounds count unless the market says Regulation only.

A player market is void if the named player does not participate. A market may state a minimum-round participation requirement.

9.5.Dota 2 and League of Legends

A map is complete when the official game record declares a winner, including a valid surrender.

First Blood counts the first opposing hero kill. Kills by neutral units or allied units do not count.

Race to Kills, map kills, map duration, objective and player markets use the official game statistics.

9.6.Valorant

Map winners, round handicaps, round totals and player-kill markets use the official Valorant match record.

Overtime rounds are included unless the market says Regulation only. A remade map replaces the abandoned map for settlement unless the competition declares another result.

9.7.Virtual map advantage

A map awarded before play under tournament rules is not counted in map totals or numbered-map markets unless the market clearly includes it.

10

Cash Out and settlement questions

10.1.Availability

Cash Out is an optional offer for selected active tickets. It may appear, change, pause or disappear as the event develops.

10.2.Confirmation

Selecting Cash Out sends a request. The ticket is settled early only when BlazeBet confirms the request and credits the confirmed amount.

The final event result does not change a correctly confirmed Cash Out.

10.3.Offer changes

The amount may change between display and confirmation. BlazeBet may reject the request or ask the Customer to try again at the updated amount.

10.4.Errors

An obvious Cash Out pricing or technical error may be corrected. BlazeBet may restore the original ticket, void only the affected ticket, or recalculate the Cash Out using the correct market information, whichever produces a fair and supportable settlement.

10.5.Settlement questions

A Customer who believes a ticket was settled incorrectly should use the official support channel within 30 calendar days and provide the ticket number and relevant evidence.

BlazeBet may pause the disputed amount while official records are checked. The Customer will retain a reasonable route to challenge the final decision.
